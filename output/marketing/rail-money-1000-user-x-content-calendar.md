# Rail Money 2-Week X Campaign

Goal: acquire the first 1,000 real TestFlight users by selling Rail Money as financial intelligence for income, not as another finance app.

Campaign idea: Give your income a default route.

Core promise: Every deposit becomes spend money and future money automatically.

Primary CTA: Join the Rail Money TestFlight beta.

Secondary CTA: Reply "RAIL" for the beta link.

## Acquisition Targets

To reach 1,000 real users, the content calendar must create distribution, not just posts.

Working funnel target:

| Stage | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Meaningful X impressions | 150,000-250,000 |
| Profile / landing page visits | 6,000-10,000 |
| TestFlight link clicks | 2,000-3,000 |
| Successful beta installs | 1,000 |

Daily minimum activity:

- 3 original posts per day from the Rail account.
- 1 founder post per day from the founder account.
- 30-50 useful replies per day under relevant posts.
- 10-20 direct warm DMs per day to people who engage.
- 1 demo clip every 2-3 days.
- Promote the top 2-3 organic posts after 24 hours if they show strong saves, replies, profile visits, or link clicks.

## Positioning Rules

Do:

- Say: "Financial intelligence for your income."
- Say: "Give your income a default route."
- Say: "70% spend. 30% stash. Automatically."
- Show the product mechanic repeatedly.
- Use salary, payday, freelance payout, remittance, and stablecoin deposit moments.

Do not:

- Lead with "send/receive/swap" as the main story.
- Sound like a generic budgeting app.
- Overpromise returns.
- Use vague AI language like "smart finance" without explaining the rule.
- Change CTAs every day.

## Reply Playbook

Use replies to distribute the campaign. Do not spam links first.

Reply style:

- Acknowledge the person's point.
- Add a useful money insight.
- Mention Rail only when relevant.
- Link only if they ask or the thread is clearly about testing.

Example replies:

> This is exactly why we think income needs a default route. Most people do not need more tracking after the money is gone; they need the first move to happen automatically.

> We are testing this with Rail: deposit lands, part stays spendable, part moves to stash. The goal is to reduce the first decision after payday.

## Kill / Scale Rules

Scale a post if:

- It gets 2x normal engagement in 24 hours.
- It drives link clicks from non-followers.
- Replies show real product intent.
- People ask for the beta link.

Kill a lane if:

- It gets likes but no clicks.
- It attracts low-intent comments only.
- It confuses users about what Rail does.
- It makes Rail sound like a generic wallet.
